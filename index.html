📋 Team Registration & Import Guide

You can collect team rosters using a simple Google Form, an Excel sheet, or by just copying and pasting a member list below each team name!

METHOD 1: GOOGLE FORM → GOOGLE SHEETS (RECOMMENDED)

Create a Google Form with these questions in order:

1. Team Name
2. Pool (Pool A / Pool B)
3. Captain Name (Male/Female)
4. Male Player 2
5. Male Player 3
6. Male Player 4
7. Female Player 1
8. Female Player 2

When responses come in, export to Google Sheets, copy the 8 columns, and paste directly into the importer!

METHOD 2: SIMPLE TEAM & MEMBERS TEXT LIST

Just type or paste each team name and members below it:
